# Security Governance Portfolio

## Overview

This portfolio showcases my work in cybersecurity governance, risk management, compliance (GRC), and information assurance. These projects demonstrate my ability to translate cybersecurity frameworks, regulatory requirements, and security best practices into practical organizational policies.

## Featured Projects

### 1. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Developed a comprehensive security policy for a simulated credit union to protect sensitive financial information and establish appropriate technology usage requirements.

Key areas:
- Identity and access management
- Endpoint security and device management
- Security awareness and training
- Data protection and regulatory compliance
- Policy exceptions and enforcement

[View Acceptable Use Policy](Acceptable_Use_Policy.pdf)

### 2. Cybersecurity Incident Response Policy (IRP)

Developed an incident response policy for a simulated tax services organization, establishing procedures for detecting, investigating, containing, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.

Key areas:
- Security Operations Center (SOC) responsibilities
- Incident classification and escalation
- Incident containment, eradication, and recovery
- Digital evidence handling and chain of custody
- Regulatory reporting and incident response metrics

[View Incident Response Policy](Incident_Response_Policy.pdf)
